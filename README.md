# Mining Playtest

Playtest builds of a medieval mining game with idle elements, by Old Men Gaming LLC. Dig down, sell your ore in the old town, reopen its buildings, and hire apprentices who keep mining while you explore.

## Play
1. Download the latest zip from the [Releases](../../releases/latest) page (Windows 10/11).
2. Unzip it and run `Mining-Playtest.exe`.
3. If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The build isn't signed yet.

Your progress saves by itself, in `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Mining Playtest`.

## Feedback
Found a bug, got stuck, or have an idea? Click **Send feedback** on the title screen or in the Esc menu. It opens a report here with your build and where you are filled in; just add what happened and what you expected. You can also [open an issue](../../issues/new) yourself.
